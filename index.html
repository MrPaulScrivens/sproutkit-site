For parents walking into an IEP meeting

Most of your IEP meeting gets decided in the two weeks before you walk in

There is a date on your calendar and a packet you have read four times. The draft usually exists days before you get there, which means the ninety minutes you are dreading is a revision session. The question is whose paper gets revised into it.

Start with The First MeetingFree. Print it, fill it in, take the folder.

Who ends up here

Two parents end up here

One of them just got the call. Somebody at the school said the word evaluation, the meeting is a week out, and the thing they are most worried about is being the difficult parent.

The other one has done three of these. They know what FAPE means, they can read an IEP front to back, and they keep going home with a document that says what it said before they walked in.

Both of them name the meeting itself as the worst part. The first because they do not know what is coming. The second because they do.

What is written here works on the two weeks around that meeting. What to ask for before the door opens, what to say while you are still in the room, and what to put in writing within two days.
