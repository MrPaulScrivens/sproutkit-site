Privacy

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and carries no third party tracking scripts.

There is no account to make and no login. You can read every page here without giving anything up.

Some pages offer to send you material in exchange for an email address. No email provider is connected yet, so at the moment nothing is collected. Once one is connected, an address you give us is used to send you the material you asked for and occasional writing, and every email carries an unsubscribe link.

Paid material is sold through an external checkout. That purchase happens on the provider's own site under the provider's own privacy policy, and your payment details never reach this site.

We do not sell or share your email address.

Questions about any of this, including a request to delete an address we hold, can go to our contact address.

Before you use any of it

SproutKit is information, not legal advice. Every legal claim carries the federal regulation it comes from so you can read it yourself.
